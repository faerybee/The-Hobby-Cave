New York

← Back to Destinations

New York City is overwhelming, electric, and completely unforgettable. In a single day you can eat a bagel in Brooklyn, see a Broadway matinee, stroll the High Line, and watch the skyline light up from the Top of the Rock — all before midnight.

Free (bike ~৳1,650/hr)
