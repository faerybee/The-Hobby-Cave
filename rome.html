Rome

← Back to Destinations

Rome is an open-air museum where 2,700 years of history surround you at every corner. From the roar of the Colosseum to the whisper of the Trevi Fountain, from piping-hot pizza al taglio to slow espresso at a pavement café — Rome seduces every sense.
